# Whole-draft refinement

Keep citations integrated with drafting, then review them together here. Read the shared principles in [writing.md](writing.md). The author may request this stage before figures are finished; refine available text and bibliography with placeholders, then recheck affected content when images arrive.

## Three citation checks

1. **Missing support:** read paragraph by paragraph for externally supported background judgments, prior methods, and specific reported findings that need citations but lack them. Add appropriate support. Do not impose a quota or cite every sentence indiscriminately.
2. **Authenticity and applicability:** verify that every cited work exists; check applicable title, authors, year, venue, and DOI or other identifiers against publisher pages, official paper records, or actual originals. Inspect the relevant primary text to confirm it supports the attached statement. Existence or topic similarity alone is not a verified citation. Record inaccessible or unresolved checks accurately and do not claim they passed.
3. **Format:** apply the actual template and venue's reference conventions. Check citation keys against bibliography entries, consolidate accidental duplicates, and fix missing fields, undefined references, and actual formatting issues.

Use authoritative sources appropriate to the work; preprints and publications may differ, so identify the version actually supporting the claim. If a reference cannot be verified, correct the source, find a verified alternative, or revise the unsupported statement. Ask the author only when their judgment or unavailable material is necessary.

## Read in final manuscript order

Read from beginning to end as a target human reader. Apply the same principles used in drafting: research argument, direct evidence-supported statements, clear English, selected detail, and progressively established understanding.

Repair repetition, jumps between sections, missing setup, inconsistent terminology, and overloaded sentences. Check that the actual abstract, introduction, findings, and interpretation agree. Review essential numbers and statistical conventions against the technical reports and underlying materials; check figure/table descriptions and captions when present. Preserve useful specificity while simplifying prose.

Fix manageable issues directly, rather than delivering only an audit list. Do not turn the final review into a request to add every possible experiment or a series of defensive disclaimers. Substantive unsupported claims need correction or a precise author question; hypothetical objections do not automatically require more paper content.

## Format and delivery

Check headings, notation, equations, figure/table numbering, cross-references, bibliography, and actual page layout. Compile with suitable available tooling and inspect the output visually. Resolve real errors and material readability issues; report an actual build limitation if no valid check is possible.

Recheck the maintained main file through the editing route chosen during template preparation; use [latex.md](latex.md) for capability checks and fallbacks. An open tab or an older PDF is not evidence that the latest source compiled. Keep the native compiler result, any separately built PDF, and observed live preview status distinct in the short delivery record.

Deliver the first draft and editable manuscript/figure sources, plus a short statement of significant outstanding matters and the next action. If the author deferred images, explicitly distinguish completed text refinement from pending figure work, retain the placeholders, and make returning straightforward.

When images are added later, check their captions, references, values, attached claims, and placement. Revisit other text only when a real content change affects it. Human verification and refinement remain part of turning this first draft into a submission.
